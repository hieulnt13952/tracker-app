1  ·  Inputs & Assumptions
Purchase priceLink to property
Down payment %Minimum in Canada: 5% to $500K, 10% on the portion $500K–$1.5M, 20% over $1.5M (insurance unavailable above $1.5M).
Down payment $Your deposit with the offer (usually 3–5% of price) forms part of this — it is credited to you on closing, but you need it as certified funds within 24 hrs of an accepted offer.
Mortgage before insurance premium
Loan-to-value (LTV)Above 80% = mortgage default insurance is mandatory.
Amortization (years)Insured max is 25 yrs, except 30 yrs is allowed for first-time buyers and for newly built homes. Longer term = lower payment, more total interest.
Mortgage interest rateThe Mortgage Calculator tab holds this as a percent in its own linked cell for the payment-by-payment schedule.
Property in City of Toronto? (Yes/No)Toronto charges a second, municipal land transfer tax on top of the provincial one.
First-time buyer(s)? (Yes/No)Drives the land transfer tax refunds below.
Newly built / substantially renovated home? (Yes/No)Resale homes have no HST on the price. New builds do — but see the FTHB GST/HST rebates in section 4.
Household gross annual incomeUsed for the stress test / GDS-TDS check in section 6.
Other monthly debt payments (car, loans, cards)Lenders count 3% of credit-card and line-of-credit balances per month even if you pay in full.
2  ·  Mortgage Default Insurance (CMHC / Sagen / Canada Guaranty)
Insurance required?Only required when the down payment is under 20%. With 20%+ down the premium and its PST are both $0.
Base premium rate (by LTV band)80.01–85% LTV = 2.80%; 85.01–90% = 3.10%; 90.01–95% = 4.00%. Source: CMHC premium tables.
Surcharge — amortization over 25 yearsCMHC applies a 0.20% premium surcharge for amortizations beyond 25 years.
Total premium rate
Insurance premium (added to the mortgage)Rolled into the loan — you pay interest on it for the whole amortization.
Ontario PST on the premium (8%) — CASH at closingCannot be added to the loan — must be paid in cash on closing. Carried into section 3.
Total mortgage amount (incl. premium)Matches Total Borrow on the Mortgage Calculator tab whenever down payment is 20%+.
3  ·  One-Time Costs to Close & Move In
Land transfer taxes
Ontario land transfer taxMarginal brackets: 0.5% to $55K, 1.0% to $250K, 1.5% to $400K, 2.0% to $2M, 2.5% above $2M.
Less: Ontario first-time buyer refund (max $4,000)Max $4,000 per transfer. Fully covers LTT on homes up to $368,000.
Toronto municipal land transfer tax (MLTT)Mirrors the provincial brackets to $2M; Toronto adds steeper luxury tiers above $3M.
Less: Toronto first-time buyer rebate (max $4,475)Stacks with the provincial one. Full rebate if price is under $400,000.
Due diligence & professional fees
Home inspection$400–$700. Budget for 2–3 if you lose bids on your first offers.
AppraisalOften waived or paid by the lender/broker on insured deals — ask.
Status certificate (condo / POTL only)Capped at $100 + HST in Ontario. Set to 100 if buying a condo.
Legal fees & disbursements$1,800–$3,000 all-in including searches, registration and HST.
Title insuranceOne-time, $250–$700 depending on price.
Survey (only if title insurance is declined)$1,000–$2,000 if actually required.
Specialty inspections (septic, well water, WETT, sewer scope, radon, pest)A general inspection excludes these.
Taxes & closing adjustments
PST on mortgage insurance premium (from section 2)$0 whenever the down payment is 20% or more.
Property tax / utility / fuel adjustments to sellerOften $500–$2,000 — easily the most commonly forgotten line.
Interest adjustment (closing date to first payment)Interest for the gap between funding and your first regular payment date.
HST on a new build, net of FTHB rebatesIf you are a FTHB on a new build under $1M, federal + Ontario FTHB rebates can wipe out the full 13%.
Tarion enrolment, development charges, levies (new builds)New construction only — can run $10,000–$25,000+ if your APS does not cap them.
Move-in & set-up costs
Moving expensesDIY cube van is cheap; full-service movers in the GTA run $1,500–$3,500.
Utility hook-ups, account set-up & deposits
Lock change / rekeyDo it on day one.
Condo move-in fee / elevator booking depositCondos only — typically $200–$500.
Immediate cleaning, paint & minor repairsDo this before the furniture arrives.
Furniture & appliancesYear-one furniture and appliances.
Window coverings, basic tools, lawn/snow gearBlinds for a whole house is a genuine surprise expense.

4  ·  Available Cash & Funding Sources
Chequing accountBe realistic — do not count money already committed to rent, bills or credit card balances.
Savings account / HISA
GICs & other cash equivalentsOnly count these if they mature before closing.
Non-registered investments (stocks, ETFs, mutual funds)Enter the after-tax amount.
Other liquid savings
FHSA — buyer 1$8,000/yr, $40,000 lifetime. Tax-deductible in, tax-free out.
FHSA — buyer 2Each partner needs their own account — no joint/spousal FHSA.
RRSP Home Buyers' Plan — buyer 1Up to $60,000 per buyer. Repaid over 15 years.
RRSP Home Buyers' Plan — buyer 2FHSA and HBP can both be used for the same purchase.
TFSAWithdrawals are tax-free; room comes back Jan 1 of the following year.
Gift from familyLenders require a signed gift letter, in your account ~15 days before closing.

Credits & rebates to claim
Ontario land transfer tax refund (already netted in section 3)Max $4,000. Claimed at registration by your lawyer.
Toronto MLTT rebate (already netted in section 3)Max $4,475, stacks with the provincial refund.
First-Time Home Buyers' Tax Credit (HBTC)$10,000 non-refundable credit amount = $1,500 back. Cash comes at tax time, not closing.
Federal FTHB GST/HST rebate (new builds only)Up to $50,000 — 100% of federal GST on a new home up to $1M, phased out to $1.5M.
Ontario FTHB HST rebate (new builds only)Up to $80,000 of the 8% provincial HST portion.

Condo parking & locker fees
Pest control, chimney & dryer vent, seasonal servicingSkipping these is what turns maintenance into a capital expense.
